# BabyCenter

Competitor profile for APP-001. All sources were accessed on 22/10/2026 (America/Sao_Paulo). The labels follow the [Vault conventions](../../00_Inicio/Convencoes.md): **[DOC]** indicates something stated on a website, store, help page or in terms; **[HIP]** indicates a hypothesis, inference, lack of mention or isolated report. Nothing was observed at runtime. English passages in quotation marks are verbatim. The codes BC01, BC02… refer to the Sources table at the end of the profile. Related profiles: [Kinedu](Kinedu.md), [Pathways](Pathways.md), [Peanut](Peanut.md); group A: [Huckleberry](Huckleberry.md), [Napper](Napper.md), [Baby Daybook](Baby_Daybook.md), [Nara Baby](Nara_Baby.md).

## Resumo

- Advertising-funded portal and app with content about pregnancy and parenting. It is the **benchmark for communities by birth month**: each "Birth Club" for 2026 has between 121 thousand and 146 thousand members [DOC BC10].
- Moderation has published layers [DOC BC05, BC06, BC07]:
  - paid staff;
  - volunteer "Group Owners";
  - "Big Sisters", temporary helpers in new clubs;
  - 10 rules;
  - reporting with a choice of recipient;
  - an appeal channel.
- The editorial content states that it receives **medical review by named reviewers**, with the author, reviewer and date at the top of the article, visible sources and a Medical Advisory Board [DOC BC13, BC15, BC16]. The community is explicitly separate: BabyCenter "doesn't monitor or verify the information posted by our members", and the Portuguese website states "Não há aconselhamento médico na comunidade nem na plataforma BabyCenter" [DOC BC05, BC11].
- **Shared caregiving was not found**: users ask in the community itself whether they can add their partner [HIP BC19, BC20].
- **Privacy is the weak point** [DOC BC01, BC04, BC17] [DOC BC21–BC23]:
  - the stores disclose sensitive and health data used in advertising;
  - the policy provides for sharing names, due dates and addresses with partners;
  - a class action filed in 22/09/2026 alleges tracking even after cookies were declined. These allegations have not yet been adjudicated.

## Features by pillar

### Routine (partial)

- Google Play mentions "Baby Growth Tracker, baby sleep log, feeding schedules and guides" [DOC BC02]. The App Store mentions "Baby Growth Tracker to chart your baby's size, development and big milestones" and logging "postpartum symptoms and notes about your baby" [DOC BC01].
- Version 6.0.0 (19/01/2026): "Calendar Timeline View: Now you can spot patterns in what you've logged". Version 6.17.0 (30/09): "daily briefing" on the Highlights screen [DOC BC01].
- The product focuses on pregnancy: kick counter, contraction timer, belly photos and "Bumpie" timeline [DOC BC01, BC02]. Diaper logging was not found [HIP].

### Development and milestones (partial)

- Milestones within the Growth Tracker and "ideas for fun baby and toddler games and activities to boost your child's development" [DOC BC01, BC02].
- The editorial content on development is extensive and medically reviewed (see tummy time). No structured activity plan like those from Kinedu and Pathways was found [HIP].

### Community (yes, flagship feature)

- **Birth Clubs** by birth month and year: 12 clubs for 2026, with 121.685 to 146.032 members each [DOC BC10]. In Brazil, the section is called "Grupos dos bebês do mesmo mês" [DOC BC12].
- **Groups by topic**, public or private. The choice "can't be changed later". Any member can create a group [DOC BC06].
  - In Brazil, examples include: "Tentando engravidar - principal" (182.321 members), "Grupo das mamães de primeira viagem" (117.801) and "Amamentação" (46.040) [DOC BC12].
- **Direct messaging** between members. The page must be refreshed to see replies [DOC BC06].
- **"Daily Digest" email** with group updates. Fixed screen name ("Once you've chosen a screen name, it can't be changed") [DOC BC06].
- **Minimal profile**: the pop-up shows the screen name, photo, number of posts and comments, and month joined. The public activity list was removed "temporarily" [DOC BC06].
- A grief and pregnancy loss group is mentioned in a positive App Store review [HIP BC01].

### Shared caregiving (not found)

- The store descriptions do not mention a family account, invitation or partner [HIP: absent from BC01, BC02].
- The community has posts from 2023 ("Baby center feature to share with partner?") and 2025 ("am I able to add my partner to the app… from his phone?"). The replies were not captured [HIP BC19, BC20].

## Educational content

- **Authorship and review** [DOC BC13]:
  - "created by our team of experienced editors working with doctors and other qualified healthcare professionals";
  - health content is reviewed by the **Medical Advisory Board** ("maternal-fetal medicine specialists, ob-gyns, midwives, lactation consultants, pediatricians…");
  - content unrelated to health goes through "editors and professional fact-checkers".
- **In the article** [DOC BC13]: "At the top of articles, you'll see the author's byline, the medical reviewer or fact-checker's name, and the date the content was last reviewed". Sources appear under the "Show sources" button.
  - Example: the tummy time article is "Medically reviewed by Chandani DeZure, M.D., FAAP" and "Written by Stephanie Watson | Nov 10, 2024" [DOC BC16].
- **Medical Advisory Board** [DOC BC15]:
  - listed specialties: obstetrics, fertility, pediatrics, emergency pediatrics, development, nutrition, dermatology, emotional health, car seat safety (CPST), among others;
  - collaborating organizations: Society for Maternal-Fetal Medicine and Hearing First;
  - names and credentials (M.D., Ph.D., RN, CNM, IBCLC) are in the page's HTML, in collapsed sections.
- **Stated references**: ACOG, AAP, SMFM, CDC, FDA, CPSC and peer-reviewed journals [DOC BC13].
- **Review frequency**: no fixed schedule. The page says "continuously review" and "If an error is discovered, we fix it immediately" [DOC BC13].
- **Editorial independence**: ads are labeled and sponsored content is marked. Product recommendations may earn commissions [DOC BC13].
- **Boundary with medical advice** [DOC BC18, BC05, BC11, BC19, BC20]:
  - terms: "YOU SHOULD NOT RELY ON THIS INFORMATION AS A SUBSTITUTE FOR… PROFESSIONAL MEDICAL ADVICE";
  - community guidelines: "advice and opinions related to medical matters, is the responsibility of the members who create the posts";
  - every post displays "Views expressed in the Community are solely the opinions of participants, and do not reflect those of BabyCenter";
  - the Brazilian website clearly separates the two: medical content "contém declaração de autoria e atribuição de revisão por especialistas", while group content "não passa por revisão editorial".
- **Content license**: the terms grant a "personal, revocable, limited, non-exclusive… non-transferable" license for personal use only. They prohibit "Copy… distribute… reproduce, publish, license, create derivative works". Reusing content **is not permitted** without an agreement [DOC BC18]. The terms also say that Everyday Health "may remove or alter any user-created content at any time for any reason" [DOC BC18].

## Tummy time

- There is **editorial content**, without a tool [DOC BC16]:
  - the article "Everything you need to know about tummy time" includes sections by age (newborn to 6 months), videos and tips (toys, supporting the baby on a cushion or blanket, "Tune in to your baby");
  - it recommends including tummy time in the routine "for the first six months".
- **Supportive tone**, the best in the group on this point: "If your little one doesn't like tummy time, it's okay. Try to do the best you can, and healthy babies will still reach their motor developmental milestones appropriately." (Dr. Chandani DeZure) [DOC BC16].
- Tummy time timer, reminder or goal in the app: not found [HIP: absent from BC01, BC02].

## Community and moderation

- **Rules** ("Last updated: January 24, 2025"). The 10 rules prohibit [DOC BC05]:
  - personal attacks;
  - racism and hate speech;
  - attacks on sexual orientation or gender identity;
  - **medical misinformation**;
  - deliberate disruption of the community;
  - bullying and harassment;
  - illegal activity;
  - pornography;
  - requesting or revealing personal data and private content;
  - advertising outside designated spaces.

  The page summarizes: "We do not tolerate racism, discrimination, and medical misinformation."
- **Responsibility**: "Because our Community is so large, BabyCenter doesn't monitor or verify the information posted by our members. We rely on you to keep us informed of violations." [DOC BC05]. The "about" page says that "professional moderators enforce our Community guidelines and review all content reported to BabyCenter staff" [DOC BC14].
- **Who moderates** [DOC BC06, BC07, BC08]:
  - "a team of paid BabyCenter staff members", identified by the "BabyCenter Staff" badge;
  - volunteer **Group Owners**, who "only have the ability to remove posts and comments in their own groups";
  - **Big Sisters**: "temporary helpers for Birth Clubs in the first trimester… experienced Group Owners chosen by BabyCenter staff", who provide guidance and also enforce the rules;
  - the team page introduces moderators by screen name and background (mothers who joined as members).
- **Group Owners guide** [DOC BC07]:
  - PERRC method (Participate, Educate, Redirect, Remind, Collaborate);
  - for heated topics, temporarily lock the thread and explain which rule applies;
  - short, standardized private messages ("Your thread was removed for violating [guideline X]");
  - remind users to use "Block, Ignore, and Report This";
  - threats of suicide or abuse go to the staff ("BabyCenter has a protocol"), with a resources page.
- **Reporting** [DOC BC06]:
  - gray flag on posts and comments: choose the type of abuse, the **recipient ("Community staff, Group Owner or Both")** and a comment;
  - profiles, messages and groups are reported only to staff;
  - anything without a report button is reported by email.
- **Sanctions and appeals** [DOC BC05]:
  - "BabyCenter reserves the right to ban any member at any time for any reason, with or without warning";
  - appeal by email, with screen name, link and justification; "Messages with profanity will result in a permanent ban";
  - channel for reporting illegal content that cites the European DSA;
  - additional group rules are not enforced by staff.
- **Crisis resources**: a page covering emergencies, breastfeeding, child abuse, domestic violence, mental health and suicide prevention, focused on the US (911, US helplines) [DOC BC09].
- **Brazil** ("Regras da comunidade", modified on 13/08/2026 according to the page metadata) [DOC BC11]. Adds local rules:
  - "Não indique remédios e tratamentos médicos";
  - "Não crie perfis falsos ou múltiplos perfis";
  - "Não peça doações";
  - "Por motivos legais, não faça críticas negativas especificando nomes de médicos ou estabelecimentos de saúde";
  - "É proibido anunciar ou fazer qualquer transação (doação, venda, troca) envolvendo medicamentos e fórmulas infantis";
  - appeals through br_community@babycenter.com;
  - notice: "com exceção dos grupos fechados, tudo o que é publicado na comunidade do BabyCenter é público… [e pode] ser mostrado por mecanismos de busca".
- **Age and verification**: the terms say that the service is not intended for anyone under 18, and registration asks for confirmation of 18+. The policy says that anyone under 16 should not use forums [DOC BC17, BC18, BC20]. Identity verification beyond email was not found [HIP].
- **Content after leaving**: "Closing your community account does not remove your content… If you place content on our site, public or private, we are not obligated to remove it." [DOC BC06].
- **Public moderation incidents**: none found in this research [HIP]. The relevant incident concerns privacy (below).

## Privacy

- **Policy** ("Effective: December 5, 2024"), administered by Everyday Health [DOC BC17]:
  - behavioral advertising and "identity partners" (hashed email for a cross-channel advertising identifier);
  - sharing with Ziff Davis companies;
  - "Consumer Health Data" section (WA/NV): "We may share your User Information with our partners such as your name, due date, postal address, email, other identifier, or inferred data";
  - opt-out of "sale" through the DSAR portal.
- **Registration**: "By continuing, you consent to the processing of your health info and data to receive emails, samples, and promotions… You agree that we may share this data with our partners." [DOC BC15, BC20].
- **App Store** [DOC BC01]:
  - "Data Used to Track You": contact information, identifiers, usage and **"Sensitive Info"**;
  - "Third-Party Advertising" includes physical address, email, name, device ID and "Sensitive Info".
- **Google Play (Data safety)** [DOC BC04]:
  - shares name, email, IDs and address for "Advertising or marketing";
  - **"Health info" shared for "App functionality, Analytics, Advertising or marketing"**;
  - encryption in transit and deletion requests.
- **Class action**: *Reimer v. BabyCenter, LLC, et al.*, no. 2:26-cv-10783, Central District of California, filed on 22/09/2026 [DOC BC21, BC22, BC23].
  - Alleges that the website sent browsing and pregnancy data to Google, LiveRamp and more than 30 companies after the user clicked "do not consent".
  - These are **allegations with no ruling or settlement**.
- **Literature**: an academic audit of 14 baby-tracking apps in Europe cites the policies of BabyCentre and What to Expect (same group) as examples of explicit disclosure of the sale and sharing of "consumer health data" [DOC BC24].

## Strengths

- Large-scale community by birth cohort, including in Brazil [DOC BC10, BC12].
- Layered moderation, publicly documented: short rules, a guide for volunteer moderators, Big Sisters when clubs start, reporting with a chosen recipient and appeals [DOC BC05, BC06, BC07].
- Explicit separation between reviewed content (author, reviewer, date, sources) and member accounts (notice on every post) [DOC BC05, BC11, BC13].
- Brazilian rules adapted to the local context: no recommending medicines, no transactions involving medicines or formula, no negative mentions of named doctors [DOC BC11].
- Nonpunitive tone about tummy time [DOC BC16].

## Limitations and recurring complaints

- Monetization through advertising and health data; pending class action [DOC BC01, BC04, BC17, BC21].
- Ads that "block part of the descriptions" (App Store review) [HIP BC01].
- No shared caregiving [HIP BC19, BC20].
- Limited postpartum routine features; the focus is pregnancy [DOC BC01, BC02].
- Public and searchable content by default (except private groups), which is not removed when the account is closed [DOC BC06, BC11].
- Gamification through reading points (Rewards) tied to brands [DOC BC01].

## What AppMaternidade can learn

- **Cohorts by stage**: birth month organizes a large-scale community well. With a small user base, broader ranges (0–3 months, 3–6 months) prevent empty groups [DOC BC10] [HIP].
- **Layered moderation with explicit roles**: paid staff with a badge, volunteer moderators with powers limited to their own group and temporary "mentors" for new groups. Publish the moderation guide [DOC BC06, BC07].
- **Reporting with a recipient choice** (staff, group moderator or both) and documented **appeals** [DOC BC05, BC06].
- **Fixed notice on each post** separating personal accounts from professional guidance, plus an explicit rule against medical misinformation and recommending medicines [DOC BC05, BC11].
- **Crisis protocol** with local resources. In Brazil, these would be national emergency and support services; the list needs its own research [DOC BC07, BC09] [HIP].
- **Visible author, reviewer, date and sources** in all educational content [DOC BC13, BC16].

## What not to copy

- Sharing health data or "due date" with advertising partners and requesting this consent during registration [DOC BC04, BC17, BC20].
- Public and searchable posts by default, and content that remains after account closure. This conflicts with the LGPD's right to erasure and exposes children [DOC BC06, BC11] [HIP].
- "Ban… for any reason, with or without warning" without proportionate criteria [DOC BC05].
- Points for reading and exchanges with brands, which turn care into engagement [DOC BC01].
- BabyCenter's brand, texts, videos and tools: the terms prohibit reproduction [DOC BC18].

## Open questions

- Does the app have diaper and sleep logging with a history, or only guides? The description mentions a "sleep log", but the research did not confirm its scope.
- Does BabyCenter Brazil use the same app? The company information page mentions the "My Pregnancy & Baby Today" app, and the App Store lists Portuguese among its languages. Is there a separate, named Brazilian medical advisory board? The Brazilian website shows "Revisado por Conselho Médico do BabyCenter" without details.
- What is the response time for reports? Is there automated moderation (filters, AI)? The sources only mention human staff.
- How does BabyCenter respond to questions about adding a partner? The replies were not captured.
- What will be the outcome of the *Reimer* case?

## Limits of this analysis

- No app was installed and no account was created. Nothing was observed at runtime. Community pages were read without logging in.
- The help pages render hidden text. They were read in the browser (textContent), and collapsed sections may have been missed.
- The class action consists only of allegations. The third-party articles (BC21, BC23) were cross-checked against the court record (BC22), but the complaint was not read.
- In the App Store, dates without a year were interpreted as 2026 and "5d ago" as around 17/10/2026 [HIP].
- Store labels are self-reported statements that the stores have not verified.
