# Kinedu

Competitor profile for APP-001. All sources were accessed on 22/10/2026 (America/Sao_Paulo). The labels follow the [Vault conventions](../../00_Inicio/Convencoes.md): **[DOC]** indicates something stated on a website, store, blog, or in terms; **[HIP]** indicates a hypothesis, inference, lack of mention, or isolated report. Nothing was observed in use. Quoted passages in English are verbatim. The codes KN01, KN02… refer to the Sources table at the end of the profile. Related profiles: [Pathways](Pathways.md), [BabyCenter](BabyCenter.md), [Peanut](Peanut.md); group A: [Napper](Napper.md), [Huckleberry](Huckleberry.md), [Baby Daybook](Baby_Daybook.md), [Nara Baby](Nara_Baby.md).

## Resumo

- Product focused on **development**: a daily plan of video activities based on age and previously recorded milestones, milestone assessment in 4 areas, and progress reports. Includes live classes with specialists and a secondary routine tracker [DOC KN01, KN02].
- Has real **shared care**: email invitations, "Main caretaker" and "Caretaker" roles, and a history of what each member did with the child. In the store, sharing appears as a **premium** feature [DOC KN02, KN06].
- The community is **peripheral**: public comments under activities and articles, interaction in live classes, and "groups" mentioned in a post from 2021. No dedicated community guidelines were found [DOC KN06, KN08, KN17] [HIP].
- There is **tension** in the tone around development. The science page says the assessment "does not evaluate your parenting" and does not diagnose. Marketing, however, uses labels such as "Ahead of pace"/"Adiantado" and percentiles compared with other children [DOC KN01, KN06, KN09, KN16].
- pt-BR is available: store, website, manual, and specialists teaching classes in Portuguese [DOC KN03, KN14, KN16].

## Features by pillar

### Rotina

- "Baby Tracker": the website says "Log feeds, sleep, and diapers" [DOC KN01, KN16]. The App Store mentions sleep and feeding [DOC KN02], and Google Play mentions sleep, feeding, and growth [DOC KN04]. The sources describe the scope differently [HIP].
- Version 5.76.0 (22/09, interpreted as 2026): the tracker began predicting the next sleep and feeding "based on their wake windows and feeding patterns". No caveats about the prediction were found in the sources consulted [DOC KN02] [HIP].
- Version 5.75.0 (03/09): sleep logging through Siri ("Start a nap with Kinedu") and dark mode "so you can use the app without the screen brightness disturbing your baby's sleep" [DOC KN02].
- Offline use: the manual from 2020 describes downloading activity videos, on iOS only. Nothing was found about offline routine logging [DOC KN06] [HIP].

### Development, activities, and milestones

- Activity plan generated from the initial assessment and renewed every 7 days based on milestones reached or not reached. Provides 4 activities per day [DOC KN06, KN08].
- Video activities, with different counts depending on the source: "1800+" on the website and "3,000+" in the store. They use household objects [DOC KN01, KN02].
- The "Change" button replaces the activity with one that is easier, harder, uses other materials, uses no materials, or covers the same area ("Surprise me!") [DOC KN06].
- After an activity is completed, the app gives a mini milestone assessment, with a "Not yet" option [DOC KN06].
- The assessment has "414 milestones. 46 skills. Four areas" (physical, cognitive, language, and social-emotional). Each skill appears in four states: "Upcoming", "Reinforce", "On track", and "Mastered" [DOC KN09].
- Versions 5.73/5.74 (ago/2026): milestone redesign with "Progress bars" and a video explaining how progress is calculated [DOC KN02].
- AI assistant "Ana" with unlimited questions on premium [DOC KN02].

### Comunidade

- Comments on activities and articles: "visible for the entire Kinedu community". Questions are answered by Kinedu's team of specialists [DOC KN06, KN07]. Kinedu 101 presents these comments as "a positive forum for parents and caregivers" [DOC KN08].
- Daily live classes on Zoom with specialists. Parents send questions through the chat, and recordings remain available [DOC KN15]. In the store: "Meet and connect with future parents like yourself during live classes!" [DOC KN02].
- The "kinedu | aprender" post (published on 16/06/2021 and updated on 07/07/2026) mentions "Grupos para compartilhar desafios e trocar dicas e experiências". Whether the groups exist in 2026 was not confirmed [DOC KN17] [HIP].
- A testimonial featured by the website itself mentions "always-on group chat" and "group therapy" [DOC KN15]. It is a user account selected by marketing [HIP].
- Forums with reporting and "Group moderators" appear in the school context (Kinedu Educators, manual from 2020), not in the app for families [DOC KN06].

### Cuidado compartilhado

- Store (2026): "Account sharing with unlimited members and the ability to add up to 5 babies", listed among premium features [DOC KN02].
- Versions 5.67.x (Oct–nov/2025): "My Family" section to "Share your account with relatives or caregivers" and switch between baby profiles [DOC KN02].
- Manual (2020, updated on 07/07/2026 in the Portuguese version) [DOC KN06, KN07]:
  - email invitation with a family role (mother, father, grandmother, nanny, etc.);
  - two user types. **Caretaker** views the plan, browses the catalog, and updates milestones. **Main caretaker** also adds and removes members, edits the child's profile, and manages the account;
  - "you can see the different members of your family and consult their activities", meaning there is a history for each member;
  - member removal through the family screen.
- The help center required login and could not be read. The search snippet says sharing the baby's profile is "one of the perks of being a Play or Learn member" [HIP KN18].

## Educational content

- **Authorship**: the blog uses the byline "Kinedu Editors — Child development specialists" (Portuguese: "Equipe editorial Kinedu — Pediatria & desenvolvimento infantil"). The articles consulted do not name a reviewer [DOC KN07, KN13]. The blog states "1,068 articles written by pediatricians, sleep coaches, and development specialists", and the footers mention "140+ Pediatric experts" [DOC KN19, KN13].
- **Class specialists**: a page with names and credentials. Includes a pediatrician (MD), an IBCLC nutritionist, sleep consultants, an occupational therapist, a speech-language pathologist, a dance instructor, and a fitness coach [DOC KN14].
- **Stated basis of the assessment** [DOC KN09, KN10]:
  - instrument created by Kinedu and tested with 2.135 Spanish-speaking caregivers;
  - review of items for 0–24 months with Michael C. Frank (Stanford);
  - study by Stanford researchers with 177.934 observations of 21.861 children;
  - replication with about 280,6 million responses from 3,5 million children (report from julho de 2026).

  This is evidence of the validity of a caregiver-report instrument, **not of the clinical effect** of the activities [HIP].
- **Marketing claims**: "partnered with Stanford University", "Recommended by Harvard's Center on the Developing Child as a parenting resource", and "recommended by pediatricians" [DOC KN02]. These are not clinical outcomes.
- **Review**: several articles display "Updated Jul 7, 2026", including texts from 2014 and 2020. The matching dates suggest a batch update, not individual medical review [DOC KN07, KN08, KN13] [HIP].
- **Boundary with medical guidance** [DOC KN09, KN10, KN11]:
  - the terms say the content does not constitute "medical or health care services or diagnoses";
  - the science page includes a box explaining what the assessment "cannot do": "Provide a diagnosis", "Evaluate your parenting", "Represent every family";
  - the model page states that it does not indicate delay, IQ, or school readiness.

  Class marketing, in contrast, promises "answers for your baby's exact situation, not generic advice" and "No more waiting for the next pediatrician appointment" [DOC KN15, KN01]. The promise brings the product closer to individual guidance [HIP].
- **Content license**: "All Content and Kinedu Algorithm shall remain the property of the Company". Texts and content are protected by copyright [DOC KN11]. No open license was found, so reuse requires permission [HIP]. User-submitted content is granted an "exclusive, perpetual, royalty-free, irrevocable, worldwide" license, including for advertising, transferable to third parties [DOC KN11].

## Tummy time

- **Content**: the article "My Baby Hates Tummy Time" [DOC KN13]
  - cites the AAP ("at least 30 minutes of tummy time per day");
  - suggests starting with 10–15 seconds and increasing gradually;
  - lists activities: bubbles, mirror, massage, on the adult's chest, visual stimulation, and "baby yoga";
  - says there are "tummy time massages routines in our app".
- **Tummy time timer, reminders, or goals**: not found. The tracker described covers sleep, feeding, diapers, and growth [HIP: absent from KN01, KN02, KN04]. Reminders exist, but are generic ("daily reminders" to do the activities) [DOC KN08].
- **Tone**: the article says tummy time "is an essential activity" and that "it is crucial to keep trying". It also claims "Prevention of developmental delays" with "Studies have shown…", without citing the study. The tone is pressuring, and the claim has no source [DOC KN13] [HIP].

## Development without competition?

Kinedu avoids a sense of competition **only in part**.

- **In favor** [DOC KN06, KN09, KN10]:
  - "each baby follows their own path and rhythm";
  - "Not yet" option;
  - "A low percentile is not a red flag";
  - "Reinforce/Upcoming" states, instead of pass or fail;
  - the science page says the data "describe children's skills, not the care they receive" and that the result is not "a permanent label".
- **Against** [DOC KN01, KN06, KN08, KN16]:
  - the free assessment shows a reading "compared to babies their exact age", with labels "Ahead of pace" and "On track" (Portuguese: "Adiantado", "No caminho certo");
  - slogans: "Stop wondering if they're on track. Know." and "Pare de se perguntar se está tudo bem. Descubra.";
  - the manual describes percentiles by skill ("completed more indicators than 65% of children of the same age");
  - reports compare with "children his/her same age".
- For AppMaternidade, the pattern to avoid is **comparison with other children as an engagement feature** [HIP].

## Community and moderation

- **Format**: asynchronous comments tied to content, plus synchronous classes (Zoom) with chat [DOC KN06, KN15]. The Learn plan's "groups" date from 2021, with current status unconfirmed [DOC KN17] [HIP].
- **Rules**: only generic "Rules of Conduct" in the terms (no defamation, hate, spam, or "trolling"). The terms warn: "We cannot ensure that other users shall or shall comply with the Rules of Conduct" [DOC KN11]. No specific community guidelines were found [HIP].
- **Reporting, blocking, and moderators**: described only for school forums, with "report a message" and "Group moderators will monitor the group" [DOC KN06]. Nothing was found for comments in the family app [HIP].
- **Identity verification**: not found [HIP].
- **Public moderation incidents**: none found in this research [HIP].

## Privacy

- **Policy** ("Last reviewed June 2026") [DOC KN12]:
  - collects the child's date of birth, sex at birth, and name (the last is optional);
  - shares with providers "to advertise on third party websites to You after You visited our Service" and uses Google Analytics;
  - mentions GDPR, CCPA, and Mexican law. LGPD is not mentioned.
- **App Store** [DOC KN02]:
  - "Data Used to Track You": location, contact information, identifiers, usage, and diagnostics;
  - "Data Linked to You" for the purpose of "Third-Party Advertising".
- **Google Play (Data safety)**: "No data shared with third parties" [DOC KN05].
  - Collects name, email, user and device IDs, photos (optional), calendar events (optional), and crash logs.
  - Encryption in transit and deletion requests.
- The two stores' disclosures differ. This may reflect a difference in how the stores define "sharing", or an inconsistency [HIP].

## Strengths

- Ready-to-use, short daily plan with household objects and easy activity changes (easier, harder, no materials) [DOC KN06].
- Explicit caregiver roles and history for each member [DOC KN06].
- Clearly written assessment limits: does not diagnose, does not evaluate parenting, does not represent all families [DOC KN09, KN10].
- pt-BR with specialist classes in Portuguese [DOC KN03, KN14].
- Voice logging (Siri) and dark mode designed for nighttime use [DOC KN02].

## Limitations and recurring complaints

- Reviews report an aggressive paywall [HIP KN04]:
  - trial pop-up "EVERY time you open the app, and there is no exit" (Google Play, response dated 07/01/2022);
  - previously free milestones became paid (2019);
  - the developer responded to both. The complaints are old, and the current store description says milestones are included in the free offering [DOC KN02].
- Slowness, a trial that did not unlock content, and fear of being charged after canceling (App Store, response dated 01/12/2025) [HIP KN02].
- Suggested activity too advanced for the child's age because it follows previously recorded milestones (App Store) [HIP KN02].
- Large rating difference between stores: 4,6 on the App Store versus 3,2 on Google Play [DOC KN02, KN04].
- Mother-centered wording: "Atenção, mamães e futuras mamães!", despite having roles for fathers, grandparents, and nannies [DOC KN03, KN06].
- Sharing between caregivers tied to paid plans [DOC KN02] [HIP KN18].

## What AppMaternidade can learn

- Place a box next to any developmental reading stating "o que isto **não** diz": no diagnosis, no judgment of the caregiver, a population that does not represent everyone [DOC KN09].
- Offer "Ainda não" as a neutral response and allow activities to be changed (easier, no materials) without penalty [DOC KN06].
- Separate the **family manager** role (invites, removes, edits the profile) from the **caregiver** role (logs and views) and show what each person did [DOC KN06].
- Tie conversations to content (comments under an activity, answered by identified specialists). This is a low-cost form of contextual community, but it requires its own rules and moderation, which Kinedu does not publish [DOC KN06] [HIP].
- Consider nighttime use: dark mode and voice logging [DOC KN02].

## What not to copy

- Comparative labels ("Adiantado", "Ahead of pace"), percentiles against other children, and anxiety slogans ("Pare de se perguntar se está tudo bem") [DOC KN01, KN06, KN16].
- Charging for the second caregiver's access. In AppMaternidade, shared care is a pillar, not an upsell [DOC KN02] [HIP].
- An exclusive and perpetual license over families' photos and comments [DOC KN11].
- Authority claims ("partnered with Stanford", "recommended by pediatricians") presented as a guarantee of results [DOC KN02].
- Brand, videos, activity catalog, texts, milestone model, and algorithm, which are proprietary [DOC KN11].
