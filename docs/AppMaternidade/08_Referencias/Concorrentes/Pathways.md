# Pathways.org (Baby Milestones app)

Competitor profile for APP-001. All sources were accessed on 22/10/2026 (America/Sao_Paulo). Labels follow the [Vault conventions](../../00_Inicio/Convencoes.md): **[DOC]** indicates something stated on a website, store, FAQ or terms; **[HIP]** indicates a hypothesis, inference, lack of mention or isolated report. Nothing was observed in use. English passages in quotation marks are verbatim. The codes PW01, PW02… refer to the Sources table at the end of the profile. Related profiles: [Kinedu](Kinedu.md), [BabyCenter](BabyCenter.md), [Peanut](Peanut.md); group A: [Huckleberry](Huckleberry.md), [Napper](Napper.md), [Baby Daybook](Baby_Daybook.md), [Nara Baby](Nara_Baby.md).

## Resumo

- US nonprofit organization focused on **early detection and intervention**. The app is **free, with no paywall**, centered on milestones with videos of real babies, activities and Tummy Timer [DOC PW05, PW06, PW01].
- It has the **most granular caregiver permission model** seen in this group: account owner, caregiver and "limited-access caregiver". The relationship can be "healthcare provider" or "daycare/child provider" [DOC PW05].
- **Routine tracking** is recent and limited: diapers and feedings, with reminders, since abril de 2026. No sleep tracking was found [DOC PW01, PW12] [HIP].
- **There is no social community** in the product. "Pathways.org Community" is the name given to the experts, parents who test the app and translators [DOC PW08] [HIP].
- **The content is not freely available for reuse** in third-party products. The terms restrict it to personal, non-commercial use and require written permission to incorporate, adapt or translate it. The brochures say "COPY FREELY, DO NOT CHANGE, MUST ACKNOWLEDGE" [DOC PW06, PW18]. Details in the "Content license and reuse terms" section below.
- **Privacy contradiction**: the stores say "never shares personal information", but the policy states "We sell personal information" and mentions sensitive inferences about the baby's health [DOC PW01, PW02, PW07].

## Features by pillar

### Routine tracking (partial)

- Since version 2.18.0 (09/04/2026): "simple trackers for diapers and feeding". The app page says "Track diapers and feedings, set reminders, and monitor weekly trends to support well-baby visits" [DOC PW01, PW12].
- Web version [DOC PW22, PW23]:
  - diapers: pee, poop or mixed, with a daily summary and weekly trend;
  - feedings: time since the last feeding, breast (minutes on each side) and bottle (oz), with history.
- Version 2.20.0 (18/08/2026) introduced "Dashboard, Feeding, and Notification Updates" [DOC PW01].
- **Sleep tracking was not found** in any source [HIP]. A home visitor reviewed the app on the App Store and said daily tracking is "fairly limited to just diapers, bottles/nursing, tummy time, and infant massage". She requested sleep, solids, mood, growth and notes, and said this would help "families that have multiple caregivers… kinship care… foster parents". The developer replied that they would pass the suggestion on to the team [HIP PW01].

### Development, activities and milestones (yes)

- Milestone and "abilities" tracker, with "real-baby video demos" of what to look for at each age [DOC PW01, PW12].
- "300+ easy activities" with video and explanation. The daily plan is based on age and milestones [DOC PW01, PW12].
- The family chooses how often activities are refreshed: "daily, every 3 days, or weekly" [DOC PW05].
- **Corrected age**: babies born 3 weeks or more before their due date have milestones adjusted until age 2 [DOC PW05].
- Stated source: "All our milestones are from CDC and AAP materials". The "abilities" were developed by Pathways experts. The activities are "created and reviewed by a team of 70+ pediatric and developmental experts" (physical therapy, occupational therapy and speech-language pathology) [DOC PW05].
- Milestone age ranges realigned with AAP well-baby visits, on a page updated on 08/01/2026 [DOC PW21].
- Content for neonatal intensive care (2.8.0, 2024), "Hot Topics" (2.17.0, fev/2026) and "Expecting Parent Content" (2.19.0, mai/2026) [DOC PW01].

### Community (not found)

- No forum, group, chat or comments between families were found in the app or on the website [HIP: absent from PW01, PW02, PW05, PW12].
- "Pathways.org Community" refers to professional reviewers, parents and caregivers who test features and provide feedback, and volunteer translators [DOC PW08, PW27].
- The visible public interaction is the organization's response to store reviews [DOC PW01, PW02].

### Shared care (yes, free)

- FAQ "How do I add a caregiver to my account?" [DOC PW05]:
  - invitation via link ("Share Invite"); the caregiver creates their own Pathways.org account;
  - **Connected With**: the owner chooses which children to share;
  - **Relationship**: "parent, other family member, healthcare provider, or daycare/child provider".
- Three permission levels [DOC PW05]:
  1. **Account owner**: "Can change any settings";
  2. **Caregiver**: "Cannot change settings, but can record progress across all areas of app and leave notes";
  3. **Limited-access caregiver**: "Can record tummy time and activity progress, but not milestones or abilities".
- The stores mention "Share progress with grandparents, caregivers, or healthcare providers". The app page says "Share Baby's profile with partners, grandparents, and caregivers so everyone stays on the same page" [DOC PW01, PW12].
- No information was found on whether each entry shows who made it, or how to revoke access [HIP].

## Educational content

- **Published editorial process** in "How We Create Trusted Information", in four steps [DOC PW09]:
  1. topics chosen with the Medical Roundtable, therapists' clinical experience, questions from social media and news;
  2. creation based on research, with AAP milestones;
  3. "multiple rounds of review", always with one round involving "feedback and approval from at least one of our 75 expert healthcare providers";
  4. "Revise, refresh, repeat!".
- **Who reviews**: "More than 75 healthcare providers", including physicians, physical therapists, occupational therapists and speech-language pathologists [DOC PW09]. The stores and FAQ say "70+" [DOC PW01, PW05]. The community page names 26 professionals with credentials: therapists (PT, OT, SLP, developmental therapy) and a researcher (PhD). No physician appears on this list [DOC PW08].
- **Medical Roundtable**: "leading physicians, therapists, clinicians, nurse practitioners, and lay advisors". The names did not appear in the capture, only the titles "Co-Chairs" and "Members" [DOC PW10] [HIP].
- **References**: CDC and AAP for milestones. The tummy time pages cite the AAP and link to healthychildren.org ("back to sleep, tummy to play") [DOC PW05, PW15].
- **Review frequency**: no stated schedule. Reviews take place when the AAP changes recommendations, new research is published or feedback arrives. Example: realignment of age ranges in 08/01/2026 [DOC PW09, PW21].
- **Boundary with medical advice** [DOC PW01, PW05, PW06, PW15]:
  - stores: "If your child is missing milestones, consult your healthcare provider. This app does not replace medical care.";
  - FAQ: "discuss this with Baby's healthcare provider. Remember to adjust for prematurity";
  - terms: "not intended to be, nor is it, medical advice" and "Pathways.org does not employ medical personnel in their national office";
  - topic pages have a "When to Get Support" section (example: if the baby consistently does not tolerate tummy time, talk to a healthcare provider).
- **Institutional tone**: early detection mission ("Catching a delay early can change the trajectory of a baby's life", on the donation page). This framing is useful, but may increase parental vigilance [DOC PW11] [HIP].

## Content license and reuse terms

Central section for APP-001. **This is not a legal opinion.**

- **Terms of use** ("Last updated: February 7, 2025") [DOC PW06]:
  - "The Services are made available for your personal, non-commercial use only." The materials (text, images, video, audio, code) belong to Pathways.org and its licensors;
  - "distribute, transmit, display, reproduce, modify, create derivative works" is prohibited. Exception: printing one copy for personal, non-commercial use. Other uses require "prior written permission", requested through "Contact Us";
  - "encourages the dissemination of its copyright materials for educational purposes only. No for-profit use by others is authorized unless approved by Pathways.org in writing";
  - links are allowed, but only as text, without a logo or suggestion of endorsement. Without written permission, users may not "frame any of the content… nor incorporate into another website or other service any intellectual property of Pathways.org". Also: "we do not permit other sites to copy and reuse information from this Site other than for educational purposes";
  - prohibits "translate, adapt" and the use of scrapers or crawlers;
  - claims copyright over "selection, arrangement, compilation… including information in the public domain".
- **Community page**: "With permission of Pathways.org, our content is free to use and distribute. Email us at friends@pathways.org to obtain permission." [DOC PW08].
- **Brochures (PDF)**: footer "COPY FREELY, DO NOT CHANGE, MUST ACKNOWLEDGE… Permission to cite any part of this work must be obtained from Pathways.org. Materials are provided at no cost; no fees or charges may be associated with any of the Pathways.org materials without prior written approval." [DOC PW18, PW19]. The same "no fees" clause appears in the stores [DOC PW01, PW02].
- **Translations**: produced by volunteers and reviewed by professional translators. They are available on the website, "where anyone can download and share it for free" [DOC PW20].
- **Interpretation for AppMaternidade** [HIP, subject to legal validation]:
  - **allowed without asking**: linking to Pathways.org pages in text, without a logo or suggestion of partnership;
  - **requires written permission**: incorporating excerpts, videos, milestone lists or activities into the app; translating or adapting; placing material behind a subscription or advertisement ("no fees or charges" clause);
  - **even for quotations**, the brochures request permission. The legal limits of quotation in Brazil (right of quotation) require a separate assessment;
  - **recommended approach**: write original content based on primary sources (AAP, CDC and Brazilian references), with professional review, and only then request a formal license from Pathways (friends@pathways.org) if any specific material is needed. Pathways' structure and compilation should not be copied, because the organization claims rights over the compilation.
- **User contributions** (feedback, "Report a Bug", emails): are fully assigned to Pathways.org ("assignment… of all right, title and interest") [DOC PW06].

## Tummy time

- **What they offer** [DOC PW01, PW13, PW14, PW15, PW16, PW17, PW18, PW20, PW28]:
  - **Tummy Timer** in the app and on the website, with manual entry ("Add New Time Entry"), history and a weekly chart (scale from 0 to 60 minutes). Shows minutes and seconds since version 2.12.0 (abr/2025);
  - home screen **widget** (iOS and Android, since 2.13.0, mai/2025) in three styles: "Tap to start the timer", "Track daily Tummy Time to fill the stars" and "See Baby's weekly wins and work toward new goals as they grow!";
  - **daily goal by age**: "Use the Tummy Timer to meet the daily Tummy Time goal for Baby's age";
  - **month-by-month videos** (2 weeks to 6 months), positions, tips for babies who dislike it, reflux and safety ("firm, flat surface", supervision, "back to sleep");
  - brochure, including in Portuguese, and paid course (US$ 20).
  - Limited-access caregivers can log tummy time [DOC PW05].
- **Time recommendation** [DOC PW15, PW13, PW16]:
  - start with 2–5 minutes a few times a day, only when the baby is "awake, alert, and calm", and stop if the baby becomes fussy;
  - reach 15–30 min/day at 2 months and "at least 60 minutes a day by 6 months";
  - **internal inconsistency**: the video page says "By 3 months of age, baby should be doing 1 hour of Tummy Time a day… Tummy Timer to make sure baby is meeting this goal".
- **Tone**: mixed.
  - Supportive [DOC PW15]: "that's completely okay", "Every minute counts, no matter where you start", "It's normal for Baby to fuss at first", "stop if Baby gets fussy to keep the experience positive".
  - Pressuring [DOC PW13, PW14, PW15]: "Tummy Time is crucial for every baby's development… More is always better!", stars to fill and "weekly wins". The timer notice can be hidden ("Hide this").
  - The effect of gamification on parental anxiety was not measured [HIP]. In the Peanut community, there is a report of guilt over not reaching "1hr of tummy time a day" (see [Peanut](Peanut.md)) [HIP].

## Community and moderation

Not applicable: no community between users was found. The "User Submissions" rules in the terms prohibit defamatory, harassing or rights-infringing content, but concern feedback sent to the organization, rather than conversations between families [DOC PW06].

## Privacy

- **Policy** ("Effective Date: February 6, 2025") [DOC PW07]:
  - collects "Health-related information, such as anticipated due date, baby milestones, activities completed, and birth information, such as duration of hospital/neo-natal intensive care unit (NICU) stay";
  - shares "To third parties for marketing purposes";
  - **"We sell personal information and process personal information for targeted advertising purposes… It may also include sensitive inferences about you and/or your baby's health."** For Nevada residents: "We sell certain covered information";
  - Google Analytics "generally anonymized"; does not knowingly collect data from children under 13, but processes the child's data provided by parents;
  - deletion through the app within 30 business days [DOC PW07, PW24]. The LGPD is not mentioned [HIP: absence].
- **Stores**: the description states "Pathways.org protects your privacy and never shares personal information" [DOC PW01, PW02]. This **contradicts the policy**. One hypothesis is that the "sale" refers to cookies and website browsing (the policy's example cites IP and "browsing data"), but the policy does not distinguish the website from the app [HIP].
- **App Store**: "Data Linked to You" for Analytics, Product Personalization and App Functionality (contact information, user ID, usage, diagnostics, "Other Data Types"). There is no "Data Used to Track You" section or Health category, although the policy mentions health data [DOC PW01] [HIP].
- **Google Play (Data safety)** [DOC PW03]:
  - shares "App interactions" for "Analytics, Advertising or marketing";
  - collects photos (optional), name, email, "Other info", device IDs, interactions, crashes and diagnostics;
  - encryption in transit and deletion requests.

## Strengths

- Truly free, with no paywall, maintained by a nonprofit organization [DOC PW05, PW06].
- Role-based permissions with a "limited access" level, useful for a nanny or daycare to log tummy time and activities without changing milestones. The "healthcare provider" relationship is also supported [DOC PW05].
- Corrected age for premature babies and milestone ranges aligned with routine checkups [DOC PW05, PW21].
- Explicit editorial process, with named reviewers and a "When to Get Support" section [DOC PW08, PW09, PW15].
- One-tap tummy time widget. The FAQ explains the Android limitation (power saving) and the fallback, which opens the app [DOC PW14].
- Brochures in Brazilian Portuguese [DOC PW20].

## Limitations and recurring complaints

- Limited routine tracking (no sleep) and logging restricted to suggested activities. One reviewer says tummy time can be logged, but not the massage suggested by the app itself, or games outside the four offered that day [HIP PW01, PW02].
- Portuguese review on Google Play: "since the last update… information is not saved, sometimes it loses the entire history, it does not stay synced" [HIP PW04].
- "visually overwhelming" interface due to the amount of content (App Store) [HIP PW01].
- Inconsistent information: number of languages (9, 20, 21 or 30+), experts (70+ or 75) and age for the 1-hour goal (3 or 6 months) [DOC PW01, PW02, PW05, PW09, PW15, PW16].
- Privacy: declared sale of data, including health inferences, versus "never shares" in the stores [DOC PW01, PW07].
- No community found in the sources consulted [HIP].

## What AppMaternidade can learn

- Model **three access levels**: owner, full caregiver and limited caregiver (logs routine events and activities, does not change milestones or settings). Also record the **relationship** (family, nanny or daycare, healthcare provider) [DOC PW05].
- Choose **which children** to share in each invitation [DOC PW05].
- Let the family choose **how often new activities appear** (daily, every 3 days, weekly) to reduce mental load [DOC PW05].
- Use corrected age and show "when to talk to a healthcare provider" beside each milestone [DOC PW05, PW15].
- Publish the editorial process with named reviewers and the date of the last review [DOC PW09].
- Offer tummy time through a widget and manual entry, for those who forgot to time it [DOC PW13, PW14].

## What not to copy

- Tummy time gamification (stars, "weekly wins", "More is always better!") and age-based goals presented as obligations. If there is a goal, it should be optional and easy to hide [DOC PW13, PW14].
- Conflicting goals across screens (1 hour at 3 months or at 6 months) [DOC PW15, PW16].
- Store privacy promise that conflicts with the policy [DOC PW01, PW07].
- **Content, videos, brochures, milestone lists, activities, brand and logo**: only with written permission. No scraping, framing or translation [DOC PW06, PW18].
