# Nara Baby

Competitor profile for APP-001. All sources were accessed on 22/10/2026 (America/Sao_Paulo). Labels follow the [Vault conventions](../../00_Inicio/Convencoes.md): **[DOC]** indicates something stated on a website, store listing or FAQ; **[HIP]** indicates a hypothesis, inference or isolated report. Nothing was observed in use. Passages quoted in English are verbatim; Portuguese quotations from English sources are loose translations. The codes R01, R02… refer to the Sources table (at the end of the profile). Related profiles: [Napper](Napper.md), [Huckleberry](Huckleberry.md) and [Baby Daybook](Baby_Daybook.md).

## Resumo

- It is a routine tracker with a "calm" visual style that also tracks **the mother's pregnancy and postpartum period** (vital signs, mood, journal, self-care routines) [DOC R04, R05].
- Shared care is the best documented of the four. It offers email invitations, multiple families per account, caregiver removal by the family creator and a recommendation of **one account per person**. Every caregiver, however, has **full access** [DOC R02].
- Tummy time appears as a "routine" that can be logged [DOC R04, R05].
- **Stopped being free in 2026** [HIP R10 for the date]. It now costs US$ 9,99/month or US$ 99,99 for lifetime access, with a 7-day trial. After that, the family can only view and export data [DOC R02]. Its Google Play rating is 3,2 [DOC R05].
- The app belongs to a company that sells infant formula (Nara Organics) [DOC R01].

## Tummy time and development

**Tummy time: available as a "routine".**

- The store listing says: "Track routines like tummy time, baths, story time and more" and "Quickly see the day's routine when switching caregivers" [DOC R04, R05].
- A dedicated tummy time timer and routine reports were not detailed in the sources [HIP].
- "Developmental milestones based on CDC guidance" and "Track developmental milestones by age" [DOC R02, R04].
- Growth charts using WHO standards for children under 2 years, as recommended by the CDC [DOC R02].
- "Parenting Guides", in version 2.8.0 from 09/09/2026: "Science-based sleep and feeding guides […] Based on your baby's age and the latest guidance from the AAP and CDC". The guides appear "while you're tracking" [DOC R01, R04]. No named authors or reviewers were found [HIP: absence].

## Features by pillar

### Rotina

- Breastfeeding with a timer for each side and an indicator of the last side used. Bottle feeding by time and amount. Pumping with a timer for each side. Solids with preset foods. Photos and notes for any feeding [DOC R04, R05].
- Diapers: wet, dirty or dry, and diaper rash "com um toque" [DOC R04].
- Sleep with a timer or manually entered start and end times. Daily or weekly charts. Nap routine based on wake windows [DOC R04].
- Health: weight, length, head circumference, medical records, medication and vaccines [DOC R04].
- CSV export, available even when the app is in read-only mode [DOC R02].

### Development, tummy time, activities and content

See the "Tummy time and development" section above.

### Comunidade

Not found in the app. Social media accounts (Instagram @narababy, TikTok @narababyapp, Facebook) appear in the store listing, but they are not an in-product community [DOC R04].

### Cuidado compartilhado

- **Invitation**: in the "Family" tab, the invitee receives an email. If they already have an account, they see the invitation in the Family tab itself [DOC R02, R03].
- **Access**: "Once you add someone to your family, they have 100% full access in the app the same way that you do". There are no roles or tiered permissions [DOC R02].
- **Removal**: only the account that created the family can remove caregivers [DOC R02].
- **Multiple families** per account, each with its own children, caregivers and subscription. Supports co-parenting across two homes [DOC R02].
- The FAQ recommends that each person have their own login instead of sharing a password. Changing the password signs out all devices using that login [DOC R02].
- Each caregiver chooses which activity types they see ("Edit Activities") [DOC R02].
- Care handoff: "View baby's recent activities when caregivers switch roles" and "Hand off childcare with the most recent diaper change recorded" [DOC R04].
- In version 2.5.6 (22/04/2026): "Editing activities in app will now keep better track of your pending edits, even when fetching updates from other caregivers" [DOC R04]. This indicates handling of pending edits during synchronization.

## Quick logging

- **Live Activities**: "Stop and start tracking without unlocking your phone" [DOC R01, R04]. Version 2.8.2 (25/09/2026) fixed outdated data when returning from a Live Activity [DOC R04].
- **Apple Watch** [DOC R01, R04].
- **Siri** (since version 2.7.0, 18/05/2026): log diapers and bottle feeds; start, switch sides, pause, resume and stop breastfeeding and pumping; start and stop sleep. If any information is missing (child, type, amount), Siri asks. **Requires an internet connection** [DOC R02, R04].
- Widgets: not mentioned in the official sources consulted [HIP: absence].
- **Backdated logging**: edit the start time and date (on Android, first the time, then "Today" to change the date). It is also possible to enter the duration without using the timer: "Sometimes I just don't have my phone!" [DOC R02].
- Reported friction: the screen locks during breastfeeding and there is no option to keep it on [HIP R08].

## Ongoing activity and timers

- There are timers for breastfeeding, pumping and sleep [DOC R04]. They appear on the lock screen through Live Activities (iOS) [DOC R04].
- **Quality indicator**: the history marks timers that exceed normal limits in red, such as sleep longer than 24 h, to make correction easier [DOC R02].
- Android: no stated support for Live Updates or a persistent notification [HIP: absence].
- Offline: not documented. The only explicit mention is that Siri requires internet access [DOC R02]. Third parties state that there is no documented offline mode [HIP R10].

## History and predictions

- Trends covering a maximum of **14 days** in the app. For longer periods, the FAQ recommends exporting and analyzing the data in a spreadsheet [DOC R02].
- No algorithmic sleep prediction is stated. What it offers is **recurring nap reminders**, of two types:
  - **Wake Windows**: the next reminder adjusts to the last wake-up;
  - **Fixed Times**: fixed times.

  [DOC R02]
- The FAQ includes a table of wake windows by age and a clear caveat: "There isn't one answer that works for every child […] this is only a template. You should adjust the schedule to match your child's sleep needs" [DOC R02].

## Content by stage: authorship and review

- The age-based guides are based on "AAP and CDC" [DOC R01, R04]. No named author or reviewer was found [HIP].
- The website shows endorsements from a pediatrician (social media profile) and Cara Dumaplin ("Taking Cara Babies") [DOC R01]. These are **marketing testimonials**, not editorial review.
- Potential conflict of interest: the product belongs to an infant formula manufacturer [DOC R01]. How this affects the feeding content was not assessed [HIP].

## Privacy

- Policy: the store link is https://narababy.com/pages/nara-baby-tracker-privacy, which leads to Nara's policy (effective since 25/08/2025):
  - provides for sharing contact, device and browsing data with "Advertising Partners", mainly for website visitors and customers;
  - states that it does not "sell" data for money;
  - refers to a "Consumer Health Privacy Policy" for US states.

  [DOC R07]
- App Store label: **used to track you**: usage data. **Linked**: contact information, user content, identifiers, usage and diagnostics. **Not linked**: advertising data [DOC R04].
- Google Play Data safety: "No data shared with third parties". Collects **health data (optional)**, IDs, interactions, content, crashes, photos and personal data. Data is encrypted in transit and deletion can be requested [DOC R06]. Unlike Google Play, the App Store label does not list health data. The discrepancy was not explained [HIP].
- The account can be deleted within the app (Account → Settings → Email & Password → Delete Account) [DOC R02].

## Strengths

- Clear family model: invitations, multiple families, removal by the creator, individual login recommended and one payment for everyone [DOC R02].
- Honest FAQ about limits: 14-day trends, Siri online only, reminders as a template rather than a prescription [DOC R02].
- Backdated logging and duration entry without a timer [DOC R02].
- Flagging of abnormal timers in the history [DOC R02].
- Also tracks the mother's health (pregnancy and postpartum) [DOC R04].

## Limitations and recurring complaints

- **Switch to a subscription** after years of promoting the app as free. There are negative reviews and a 3,2 rating on Google Play [DOC R05] [HIP R05, R08].
- An expired subscription blocks new entries **for the entire family** [DOC R02].
- Every caregiver has full access. There is no observer role or limited permission [DOC R02].
- English only in the App Store [DOC R04]. Trends limited to 14 days [DOC R02].
- Android appears to lag behind iOS in Live Activities and watch support [HIP: asymmetry in the sources].

## What AppMaternidade can learn

- Email invitations, **one identity per person**, multiple families per account and removal by the administrator form the right foundation. Roles (for example, observer) and revocation with a defined effect on offline devices are missing [DOC R02].
- Seeing the recent routine when caregivers change ("switch roles") is a concrete example of a care handoff. It is worth making this an explicit shift summary [DOC R04].
- Validate data (timers longer than 24 h) and allow easy correction [DOC R02].
- Write wake-window caveats in the FAQ itself and in the interface [DOC R02].
- Monetization: blocking a family's logging for nonpayment causes backlash (reviews) [HIP R05, R08]. If charging, preserve essential logging and export.
- Commercial neutrality in feeding content: assess Brazilian rules on infant formula promotion before any partnership [HIP; legal verification pending].

## What not to copy

- Brand, name, "calm" palette, illustrations, store and FAQ text, and guide content.
- The same full access for any invited caregiver [DOC R02].
- Blocking logging for the entire family when payment expires [DOC R02].
