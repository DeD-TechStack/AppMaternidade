A wider box at 542:421 brings in the neighbouring houses and their lit windows, a new composition and not a re-export. Route: HOME-ART-REGEN (after the Codex reset of 20/11) regenerates a wider M11; the slot then grows from 542 to 720 CSS px.

Door: rows 1070..1253 are cut out of every tier: the 171 px floor strip (#3D312C / #3C302B in the master) plus the 13 rows of the light-spill top edge above it (cutting at the strip alone leaves a yellow sliver on the bottom edge), so the light spill under the door is not in these plates. The bottom row of the cropped plate is two token colours, not one: `cacau` #2B2320 (the wall, about 53%) and `noite-cartao` #3A302B (the door panel, about 46%); numbers per tier in `.oc/MEDIDAS.txt`. The wall component is snapped to `cacau` #2B2320 before the LANCZOS downscale from the cropped 1254 plate and again on each tier. Lossless on purpose: lossy VP8 cannot reproduce #2B2320 (no input colour within +-4 decodes to it at q82..q100).

Token snap (M11 and door): the table of `DIRECAO_FABLE/5_freeze/studies/_work/prep.py` (sha256 662c7cd9f8a31f951c94362baa608004e37567827d59876a2c74536ccb81d397), the one that made the served plates.
