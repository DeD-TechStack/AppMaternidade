The manifest `MANIFESTO.json` carries the capture records and the hashes.

HOME-FINAL-b recapture (2026-11-19): `historico-y-alta-*` and `hoje-n-*` were captured again by `.oc/rec.mjs`, which calls the `janela` extra of `.oc/capturar.mjs` with fail-closed asserts (demo sign-in, DPR 2, asserted paths `/app/historico` and `/app/hoje`) and encoded by `.oc/gerar.py` (PIL LANCZOS, WebP method 6, the quality per file below).
